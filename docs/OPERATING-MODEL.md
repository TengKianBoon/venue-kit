# Operating model

## Cost to run (single venue)

| Item | Cost | Ceiling before upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase Free | 0 | 50,000 monthly active users, 500 MB database |
| Netlify Free | 0 | Ample for a single-venue site (check current plan limits) |
| Google OAuth | 0 | Not applicable |
| Domain | ~Rp 150–250K a year | Not applicable |

A typical neighbourhood venue stays inside the free tiers indefinitely. The first paid step is Supabase Pro (about USD 25 a month), needed only for daily automatic backups or very high traffic.

## Who owns what

- The **venue** owns the Supabase organisation, the domain and the member data.
- The **builder** is an Administrator on the venue's project and can be removed at any time.
- This keeps the venue free of lock-in, which makes the engagement easier to agree to.

## Routine operations

| When | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| Any time | Owner | Edit promotions, menu, singers and line-up; send vouchers |
| Each visit | Staff | Scan the member QR, log the visit and spend |
| Weekly | Owner | Admin → Backup → download Excel |
| On request | Builder | New features, new venue, troubleshooting |

## How it earns its keep

- **Retention:** member vouchers, birthday-month offers and VIP targeting bring regulars back.
- **Insight:** visits and spend per member show who the regulars are.
- **Speed:** a promotion goes live in minutes, not days.

## Commercial model (builder side)

- **Setup:** a one-off fee per venue for configuration, branding, content load and training.
- **Care plan:** optional and monthly, covering changes, backups and monitoring.
- **Template leverage:** each new venue reuses the same templates and schema, so delivery cost falls with every deployment.
